Questions

Before your visit

Can't find what you need? Message us on WhatsApp.

Do I need a referral to see a specialist?

No. You can book any of our consultants directly. If you're using an HMO, some plans ask for a referral code — bring it along and we'll handle the rest.

Which HMOs do you accept?

We accept NHIA, IMSHIA and most private HMOs including Hygeia, AXA Mansard, Reliance, Leadway, Avon and Clearline. Call us to confirm your specific plan.

How much is a consultation?

General consultations start at ₦10,000 and specialist consultations at ₦15,000–₦25,000. You can pay online when booking, or at the front desk.

What should I bring to my first visit?

A valid ID, your HMO card if you have one, any previous test results and a list of the medicines you currently take.

Is the emergency unit really open 24 hours?

Yes — every day, including public holidays, with a doctor on site at all times and an ambulance on standby.
