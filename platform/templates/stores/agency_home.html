Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Does Alpha Dev build apps as well as websites?

Yes. Alongside websites, we build Android and iOS apps. We have shipped working apps for clients and scope every app project around what the business actually needs before writing a line of code.

Do you only work with clients in Owerri?

No. Alpha Dev is based in Owerri, Imo State, but we work with clients across Nigeria remotely, from the first call through to launch and support.

How much does a website cost with Alpha Dev?

Business websites start from 150,000 naira. Final pricing depends on the number of pages, whether you need a booking or enquiry system, and whether the project needs a custom platform rather than a standard site. If you just need a simple one-page store, Alpha Dev Stores starts from ₦2,000/month.

Do you help set up Google Business Profile and local search?

Yes. Google Business Profile setup and basic SEO are part of our standard packages, built for how Nigerian customers actually search for and find local businesses.

How long does a typical project take?

A standard business website usually takes one to three weeks from kickoff to launch. Platforms and custom apps are scoped individually based on complexity.

Do you offer support after the website is live?

Yes. Every package includes a post launch support window for fixes and small changes, so the site keeps working for you after handover.

What is Alpha Dev Stores?

A simpler, cheaper way to get online if a custom build is not what you need yet. Pick a look, add your details and photos, and publish a one-page site for your business, billed monthly from ₦2,000 instead of paid upfront. See if your business name is free.

What is the difference between Alpha Dev Stores and a custom website?

Alpha Dev Stores is a templated one-page site you build yourself from preset looks, built for small businesses that need to be online fast on a small budget. A custom website is designed and built by us from scratch, with as many pages and features as your business needs. Many Stores customers upgrade to a custom build once they outgrow the template.

Can I try Alpha Dev Stores before paying?

Yes. Building your store is free. You only pay when you are ready to publish it and get your live link.
