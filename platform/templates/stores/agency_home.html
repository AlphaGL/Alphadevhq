Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Does Alpha Dev build apps as well as websites?

Yes. Alongside websites, we build Android and iOS apps. We've built real apps for real clients, and we plan each one around exactly what your business needs before we start building.

Do you only work with clients in Owerri?

No. Alpha Dev is based in Owerri, Imo State, but we work with clients across Nigeria remotely, from the first call through to launch and support.

How much does a website cost with Alpha Dev?

Business websites start from 150,000 naira. Final pricing depends on the number of pages, whether you need a booking or enquiry system, and whether the project needs a custom platform rather than a standard site. If you just need a simple one-page store, Alpha Dev Stores starts from ₦3,500/month.

Do you help set up Google Business Profile and local search?

Yes. Google Business Profile setup and basic SEO are part of our standard packages, built for how Nigerian customers actually search for and find local businesses.

How long does a typical project take?

A standard business website usually takes one to three weeks from start to finish. Bigger platforms and apps take longer — we'll tell you how long before you commit to anything.

Do you offer support after the website is live?

Yes. After your site goes live, we fix any problems and make small changes for free for a set number of days.

What is Alpha Dev Stores?

A simpler, cheaper way to get online if a custom build is not what you need yet. Pick a look, add your details and photos, and publish a one-page site for your business, billed monthly from ₦3,500 instead of paid upfront. See if your business name is free.

What is the difference between Alpha Dev Stores and a custom website?

Alpha Dev Stores is a ready-made one-page site you build yourself by picking a design you like — made for small businesses that need to be online fast and cheap. A custom website is designed and built by us from the ground up, with as many pages and features as your business needs. Many Stores customers move up to a custom build once they outgrow the ready-made site.

Can I try Alpha Dev Stores before paying?

Yes. Building your store is free. You only pay when you are ready to publish it and get your live link.
