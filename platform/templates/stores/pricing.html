Questions

Good questions, straight answers.

Is the free plan really free?

Yes. No card, no time limit. You get a one-page website at yourname.alphadevhq.com with up to 5 photos, WhatsApp buttons and reviews. Free sites show a small "Made with Alpha Dev" note at the bottom.

Do you take a cut when I sell?

No, never, on any plan. Your customers pay you directly.

What happens if I stop paying?

Your site stays online on the Free plan. Extra pages and photos are hidden, not deleted, and they come back the moment you upgrade again.

Can I change plans later?

Any time, from your dashboard. Start on Free, upgrade when you need more, or switch to yearly to get 2 months free.

I don't have time to set it up. Can you do it?

Yes. On the Business plan our team sets up your whole site for you. Just send us your details and photos on WhatsApp.

Ready-made or custom: which do I need?

If you want to be found online and get WhatsApp messages, start with a ready-made site. If you need online ordering, bookings or a system built around how you work, look at our custom websites above.
